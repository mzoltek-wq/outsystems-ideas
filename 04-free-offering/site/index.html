The idea

A low-friction front door for Mentor.

This experience gives builders, prospects, and curious teams a quick way to test what OutSystems Mentor can do from the AI tools they already use. The free tier is intentionally bounded, but the output is real enough to keep exploring.

How it works

From signup to app package in one week.

01

Sign up with email

The user registers with an email address so the build can be linked to a delivery destination and follow-up path.

02

Connect any AI prompt

Codex, ChatGPT, or another AI client sends structured intent to a lightweight MCP endpoint that can reach Mentor.

03

Build inside a free window

The session lasts for a limited period, such as seven days, with clear boundaries around usage, data, and integrations.

04

Receive your OML files

When access ends, the generated OutSystems files and summary are emailed so the user can keep the work and decide what comes next.

Free experience boundaries

Useful enough to learn. Limited enough to stay simple.

The free path should feel generous without becoming a full production environment. Restrictions make expectations clear before anyone starts building.

Time-boxed access Example: seven days from signup.
Scoped Mentor actions Prompting, app shaping, packaging, and export only.
Limited external integrations Use mock data first; connect real systems after upgrade.
Transparent model usage Default AI routing for free builds, bring your own later.

After the week

The ending creates the next step.

Users get an email with their OML files, a build summary, and a clear invitation to open a full OutSystems environment. That upgrade path unlocks deeper work: own integrations, preferred AI models, governance, collaboration, and production-ready iteration.
